🛡️ Smart Emergency Response Capabilities

Engineered with robust OOP patterns for rapid Bangladesh disaster management

🔥

Crowdsourced Verification

Citizen reports start as REPORTED. Once 3+ independent citizens confirm the event, the system automatically verifies it and awards the initial reporter +5 Trust Score.

⚡

Automated 999 Escalation

Using the Observer Pattern, critical severity incidents (or score ≥ 200) instantly trigger notifications and logging to the National Emergency Service (999) simulation log.

🚒

Polymorphic Fleet Dispatch

The system evaluates team specialization via polymorphism: Fire Brigades for fires, Coast Guard for floods, and Medical Units for trauma, assigning composed equipment dynamically.

❤️

Post-Disaster Rehabilitation

Resolving an incident automatically opens a verified community relief drive with targeted BDT funding and emergency ration packages for affected survivors.

🎯

Citizen Trust Rating

Reporters hold trust scores from 0–100%. Reports from low-trust accounts (<20%) are flagged as unverified, while confirmed hoax / false alarms penalize reporters by -20 Trust.

📊

Disaster Hotspot Intelligence

Real-time incident clustering analyzes locations across Dhaka, Sylhet, Chittagong, and haor basins to generate high-risk zone alerts and average response time metrics.
